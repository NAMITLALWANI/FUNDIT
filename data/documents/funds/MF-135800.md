## Investment objective
The investment objective of the scheme is to seek long term capital appreciation by investing atleast 80% of its net assets in equity/equity related instruments of the companies in Information Technology Sector in India.However, there is no assurance or guarantee that the investment objective of the Scheme will be achieved.

## Scheme classification and risk
Tata Digital India Fund - Direct Plan - Growth Option is an open ended equity scheme managed by Tata Mutual Fund in the SEBI Sectoral/Thematic category. The SEBI Risk-o-meter level disclosed for the scheme is Very High. The data refers to the Direct Plan, Growth option.

## Investment terms
Minimum SIP instalment: Rs 100. Minimum lump sum investment: Rs 5,000. No statutory lock-in period applies. Exit load terms are not included in the public data snapshot; refer to the Scheme Information Document.
