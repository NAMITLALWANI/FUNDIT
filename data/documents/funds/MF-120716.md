## Investment objective
The principal investment objective of the scheme is to invest in stocks of companies comprising Nifty 50 Index and endeavor to achieve return equivalent to Nifty 50 Index by “passive” investment. However, there can be no assurance or guarantee that the investment objective of the scheme would be achieved.

## Scheme classification and risk
UTI Nifty 50 Index Fund - Direct Plan - Growth is an open ended other scheme managed by UTI Mutual Fund in the SEBI Index Fund category. The SEBI Risk-o-meter level disclosed for the scheme is Very High. The data refers to the Direct Plan, Growth option.

## Investment terms
Minimum SIP instalment: Rs 500. Minimum lump sum investment: Rs 1,000. No statutory lock-in period applies. Exit load terms are not included in the public data snapshot; refer to the Scheme Information Document.
