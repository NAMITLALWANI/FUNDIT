## Investment objective
To generate long term capital appreciation and income distribution to investors from a portfolio that is predominantly invested in equity and equity related securities of large cap companies. However, there can be no assurance or guarantee that the investment objective of the Scheme would be achieved.

## Scheme classification and risk
ICICI Prudential Large Cap Fund (erstwhile Bluechip Fund) - Direct Plan - Growth is an open ended equity scheme managed by ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund in the SEBI Large Cap Fund category. The SEBI Risk-o-meter level disclosed for the scheme is Very High. The data refers to the Direct Plan, Growth option.
