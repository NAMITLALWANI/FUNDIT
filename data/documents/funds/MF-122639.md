## Investment objective
To seek to generate long-term capital growth from an actively managed portfolio primarily of Equity and Equity Related Securities. Scheme shall invest in Indian equities, foreign equities and related instruments and debt securities.

## Scheme classification and risk
Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund - Direct Plan - Growth is an open ended equity scheme managed by PPFAS Mutual Fund in the SEBI Flexi Cap Fund category. The SEBI Risk-o-meter level disclosed for the scheme is Very High. The data refers to the Direct Plan, Growth option.
