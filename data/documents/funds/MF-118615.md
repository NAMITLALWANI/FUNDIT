## Investment objective
The primary objective of the Scheme is to generate capital appreciation with relatively lower volatility over a longer tenure of time. The Scheme will accordingly invest in equities, arbitrage opportunities and derivative strategies on the one hand and debt and money market instruments on the other. The Scheme may also invest in Infrastructure Investment Trusts and Real Estate Investment Trusts. However there is no assurance that the investment objective of the Scheme will be realized.

## Scheme classification and risk
Edelweiss Balanced Advantage Fund - Direct Plan - Growth is an open ended hybrid scheme managed by Edelweiss Mutual Fund in the SEBI Dynamic Asset Allocation or Balanced Advantage category. The SEBI Risk-o-meter level disclosed for the scheme is Very High. The data refers to the Direct Plan, Growth option.

## Investment terms
Minimum SIP instalment: Rs 100. Minimum lump sum investment: Rs 100. No statutory lock-in period applies. Exit load terms are not included in the public data snapshot; refer to the Scheme Information Document.
