## Investment objective
The primary investment objective of the scheme is to seek to generate capital appreciation & provide long-term growth opportunities by investing in a portfolio of Small Cap companies. There is no assurance that the investment objective of the Scheme will be realized

## Scheme classification and risk
Quant Small Cap Fund - Direct Plan - Growth Option is an open ended equity scheme managed by quant Mutual Fund in the SEBI Small Cap Fund category. The SEBI Risk-o-meter level disclosed for the scheme is Very High. The data refers to the Direct Plan, Growth option.
