## Scheme classification and risk
ICICI Prudential Balanced Advantage Fund - Direct Plan - Growth is an open ended hybrid scheme managed by ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund in the SEBI Dynamic Asset Allocation or Balanced Advantage category.
