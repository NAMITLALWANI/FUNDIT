## Investment objective
To provide investors with opportunities for long-term growth in capital along with the liquidity of an open-ended scheme by investing predominantly in a well diversified basket of equity stocks of small cap companies.

## Scheme classification and risk
SBI SMALL CAP FUND - Direct Plan - Growth is an open ended equity scheme managed by SBI Mutual Fund in the SEBI Small Cap Fund category. The SEBI Risk-o-meter level disclosed for the scheme is Very High. The data refers to the Direct Plan, Growth option.
