## Investment objective
The primary investment objective is to seek to generate long term capital appreciation from a portfolio that is substantially constituted of equity and equity related securities of small cap companies/entities. From time to time, the fund manager will also seek participation in other equity and equity related instruments to achieve optimal portfolio construction.

## Scheme classification and risk
DSP Small Cap Fund - Direct Plan - Growth is an open ended equity scheme managed by DSP Mutual Fund in the SEBI Small Cap Fund category. The SEBI Risk-o-meter level disclosed for the scheme is Very High. The data refers to the Direct Plan, Growth option.

## Investment terms
Minimum SIP instalment: Rs 100. Minimum lump sum investment: Rs 100. No statutory lock-in period applies. Exit load terms are not included in the public data snapshot; refer to the Scheme Information Document.
